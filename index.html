What's Involved?

Study Duration: Up to 1 Month

We'll ask you to share information about:

  • Your current weight
  • Your current activity level
  • General health and wellness information
  • Progress updates throughout the month

Time Commitment

10-15 minutes per weigh-in to log your activities
5-10 minutes for initial and final surveys

Benefits of Participating

  • Receive personalized progress messages throughout the study
  • Learn which daily habits and behaviors correlate with weight loss
  • Automatically be entered for a chance to $50 gift card raffle after completing the final check-in
