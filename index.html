MIDS241 Weight Check-in Study

Weigh-in Frequency Causal Study

Welcome to the MIDS241 Weight Check-in research project. This study explores the causal relationships between weigh-in frequency and health outcomes.

About the Study

This research project is part of the UC Berkeley Master of Information and Data Science (MIDS) program, specifically for the MIDS 241 course on Experiments and Causality.

Research Focus

Our study investigates the impact of different weigh-in frequencies on:

  • Weight management outcomes
  • Behavioral patterns
  • Long-term health effects
