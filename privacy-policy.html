Privacy Policy

Weight Loss Research Study

Last Updated: October 27, 2025

Your Privacy Matters

We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we collect, use, and safeguard your data during this research study.

1. Information We Collect

For the purposes of this weight loss research study, we collect the following information:

  • Email address (for communication and participant identification)
  • Weight measurements and progress data
  • Activity level information
  • General health and wellness information you choose to share
  • Survey responses related to the study

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information, including your email address, will be used solely for the purpose of this research study. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Communicate with you about the study and send reminders
  • Track your participation and progress throughout the study period
  • Conduct research analysis (in anonymized, aggregate form)
  • Send you study results and findings (if you opt in)

3. Data Protection and Security

We take your privacy seriously and have implemented appropriate security measures:

  • All personal data is stored securely on password-protected systems
  • Access to identifiable information is restricted to authorized research team members only
  • Data used for research analysis is anonymized and aggregated
  • We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission

4. Third-Party Sharing

We will NOT sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Your email address and other identifiable information will remain confidential and will only be accessible to the UC Berkeley research team conducting this study.

Anonymized, aggregate data may be used in research publications or presentations, but no individual participants will be identifiable.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your personal information, including email addresses, will be permanently deleted within 90 days of the completion of this study.

Anonymized research data may be retained for academic purposes, but it will contain no personally identifiable information.

6. Your Rights

As a study participant, you have the following rights:

  • Right to withdraw: You may withdraw from the study at any time without penalty
  • Right to access: You may request to see what data we have collected about you
  • Right to deletion: You may request deletion of your data at any time
  • Right to correction: You may request corrections to any inaccurate data

7. Academic Context

This study is being conducted as part of an academic class assignment for MIDS 241: Experiments and Causality at the UC Berkeley School of Information. While this is an educational project and not an IRB-approved research study, we are committed to treating your personal information with the highest regard and following best practices for data privacy and protection.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this privacy policy or your personal data, please contact us:

Email: midsweightresearchstudy@gmail.com

Course: MIDS 241: Experiments and Causality

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be communicated to participants via email.
